# Altitude: Aim at the Goal Beyond the Goal

Ask what the user wants to happen next. "Fix this crash" becomes "keep the app crash-free". Source: Felix Rieseberg (2026-10-13), Altitude.

- `Outcome:` the lasting state, not the task: "leadership catches problems early", not "make the QBR deck".
- Tell: the work feeds a meeting or a decision. Weak fit for creative work.
- Climb one rung at a time: the task → the decisions it serves → a better mechanism than the task, proven on the user's own history and run beside the current one.
- Altitude usually needs a workflow change first: name the access the model lacks (crash reports, version-to-commit map, machines to reproduce on) as part of `Raw:` and `Instruments:`.
- The higher outcome widens scope; say so in one line and let the user pick the rung.
